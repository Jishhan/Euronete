Why don’t you use AI or templates for creating resumes?

We don’t rely on AI or generic templates because they produce resumes that feel impersonal, generic, and ineffective. AI-generated resumes and templates often lack the human insights, strategic positioning, and personalization needed to impress hiring managers. Every resume we craft is manually written and strategically tailored by an HR professional who understands recruiter psychology and industry standards.

?

Who will actually write my resume?

Your resume will be manually drafted by an experienced HR professional with a deep understanding of applicant tracking systems (ATS), recruiter preferences, and industry best practices. You’ll be working directly with someone who’s screened thousands of resumes and knows exactly what works—and what doesn’t.

?

How long does it take to receive my resume?

Your first resume draft will typically be delivered within 3–5 business days after we’ve collected your information and discussed your goals. If you have an urgent requirement, priority delivery is available with our Premium Package.

?

Can I ask for revisions after I see my resume draft?

Absolutely! Every resume package includes at least one round of revisions. We encourage you to provide detailed feedback to ensure your resume perfectly captures your professional strengths. Our Premium Package includes up to three revision rounds for maximum flexibility and satisfaction.
